How Google AI Mode Alters the Search Journey for a Business

The short version

Google AI Mode is an updated search architecture that uses generative models to answer complex queries. Instead of returning 10 blue links, Google AI Mode performs query fan-out, breaking a single search into multiple sub-queries. The system reads the top results, compiles the facts, and displays 1 unified response at the top of the page.

Google AI Mode changes how a search engine processes user queries and generates results. The system uses the Gemini model to read multiple sources, synthesise facts, and present an answer directly on the page before listing traditional links. Australian businesses must understand this process because it alters how a customer finds local information. A standard complex query now triggers 1 AI overview, changing the traditional click path that existed in previous years.

What Google AI Mode does with a query

Google AI Mode transforms a single user input into a multi-step research task. When a user types a complex question, Google AI Mode initiates a process called query fan-out. The system breaks the main question into smaller, specific sub-queries. Google Search processes these sub-queries simultaneously across its index. The architecture retrieves documents for each sub-query, extracts the relevant facts, and passes the text to the Gemini model.

The Gemini model evaluates the extracted text for accuracy and relevance. Google AI Mode then structures a cohesive response from these verified fragments. The system places this generated response at the top of the results page. Google Search appends citation links to the text, directing users to the source websites. This process requires the search engine to perform heavy computation before the user sees the first result.

A business website must provide clear, machine-readable facts to participate in this new query journey. Google AI Mode relies on structured data, clear headings, and concise text to extract information efficiently. The system bypasses websites with ambiguous content or slow loading times. Google Search Central provides documentation on how a business can structure content for optimal extraction.

How AI Mode differs from a standard results page

A standard Google Search results page in 2023 presented a linear list of links. A user typed a query, and Google Search returned a ranked list of websites. The user clicked a link, evaluated the website, and returned to the search page if the website lacked the answer. The user performed the synthesis of information across multiple websites. The business website received traffic directly from the initial click.

Google Search AI Mode reverses this dynamic. Google AI Mode performs the synthesis on behalf of the user. The system displays AI overviews in search, presenting a complete answer above the traditional organic listings. The user reads the answer on the search page without clicking a link. The user only clicks a citation link if they require deeper context or wish to verify the source.

This shift from a standard page to AI mode vs search changes the visibility of a business. A website ranking in the first position in 2023 received a high percentage of clicks. A website featured in a Google AI Mode overview receives clicks based on the prominence of its citation. The business must optimise for inclusion in the generated text rather than solely focusing on traditional ranking signals.

What changes in Search Console when AI Mode answers

Google Search Console tracks the performance of a website in Google Search. When Google AI Mode generates a response, Google Search Console records the interaction differently than a standard click. The platform currently groups clicks from AI Overviews into the general web search performance metrics. A business cannot easily isolate traffic specifically originating from a Google AI Mode citation link.

Google Search Central indicates that impressions occur when a user scrolls the AI overview into view. If the user expands the overview and sees a link to the business website, Google Search Console registers 1 impression. If the user clicks the link within the overview, the system records 1 click. The business must monitor overall impression trends to identify shifts caused by Google AI Mode.

A business owner must analyse the query data in Google Search Console to detect changes. If a high-volume query suddenly loses clicks but maintains impressions, Google AI Mode likely answers the query directly. The business must adapt its content strategy to target queries that require deep expertise, as Google AI Mode handles basic informational queries efficiently.

Whether Australian businesses are affected yet

Australian businesses face a changing environment as Google AI Mode rolls out globally. The adoption of artificial intelligence technologies within the country provides context for this shift. The Australian Bureau of Statistics released data on 25 June 2026 detailing this adoption. The Business Characteristics Survey, which surveyed nearly 7000 Australian businesses between October 2025 and February 2026, highlights the current state of AI usage.

The Australian Bureau of Statistics reported that around 12 per cent of Australian businesses used AI in their workplace in 2024-25. Around 35 per cent of large businesses reported using AI, while 22 per cent of medium-sized businesses had adopted the technology. Uptake among small and micro businesses was lower, sitting at around 11 per cent. However, the AI adoption rate for innovation active small businesses reached 19 per cent.

These figures indicate that while large enterprises adopt AI rapidly, small businesses lag behind. A small business that understands Google AI Mode gains an advantage over competitors who ignore the technology. As Google Search integrates these features more deeply, Australian businesses must adapt their digital presence to remain visible to potential customers.

What a Perth business should check first

A Perth business must evaluate its technical configuration to ensure compatibility with Google AI Mode. The business owner should first check the robots.txt file. Google uses the Google-Extended user agent to crawl websites for training its generative models. A business can block the Google-Extended user agent if it wishes to prevent its content from training future models, though this does not remove the site from standard Google Search indexing.

The business should also implement the llms.txt specification to provide clean data to AI systems. The llms.txt file uses Markdown format and sits at the root directory of the website. The specification requires an H1 with the name of the project or site. This file allows fixed processing methods to read the core facts about the business, increasing the likelihood of accurate inclusion in Google AI Mode responses.

Finally, the business must audit its existing content for clarity and factual accuracy. Google AI Mode prioritises unambiguous, well-structured information. The business must replace vague marketing copy with precise entity triples. The website must clearly state the services offered, the locations served, and the contact methods available. Gemini can then extract the data without guessing at it.

How Google AI Mode impacts a local business in Perth

A Perth business operating across the metropolitan area must structure its location data for Google AI Mode. When a user searches for services in the northern corridor, Google AI Mode scans for entities explicitly linked to Joondalup or Wanneroo. If the user query targets the central districts, the system extracts facts from websites detailing operations in Victoria Park or Subiaco. The business website must state these local government areas clearly in the text. Google AI Mode relies on these explicit geographic references to match a local business to a hyper-local user query.

Questions about Google AI Mode

What is Google AI Mode?
Google AI Mode is a search architecture that uses generative artificial intelligence to answer complex queries. The system reads multiple documents, extracts facts, and displays 1 unified response at the top of the search page. Google AI Mode reduces the need for a user to click through multiple websites.
How does Google AI Mode affect website traffic?
Google AI Mode alters website traffic by answering informational queries directly on the search page. If a user finds the answer in the generated overview, they may not click a link. A business must track its impressions in Google Search Console to monitor these shifts across its 10 most popular pages.
Can a business block Google AI Mode from reading its content?
A business can use the Google-Extended user agent in its robots.txt file to block Google from using its content for AI training. However, blocking Google-Extended does not remove the website from standard Google Search results. The business must weigh the benefits of privacy against the risk of losing 1 potential citation source.
